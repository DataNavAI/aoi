# aoi-develop-ideas-eval

Read [the research contract](../../docs/research-contract.md), especially the evaluation protocol. Obtain the frozen task output, raw sources, and relevant prior records. Audit the following requirements and produce an evidence-linked verdict; do not merely grade the performer’s summary.

- Verify each seed article was on a captured latest Seeking Alpha Trending Analysis listing with observation time and actual listing evidence. Search snippets, other publications, and independently invented theses are not eligible discovery sources. Primary sources may validate only. Trace each idea's mechanism/direction/equity back to an accessible thesis-bearing article body; a teaser alone is insufficient. If login was needed, verify the user was asked to sign in using their browser/authorized session and confirm readiness, without collecting secrets, before an access-blocked conclusion; check the post-login retry or explicit declined/unavailable access outcome. Pending login is a blocked/incomplete run, not a passing zero-idea result. Zero new ideas is acceptable only with a complete, truthful access/duplicate outcome log, not invented substitutes.
- Inspect the durable article ledger before/after, immutable extractions and result references, stable article IDs/canonical aliases, and atomic claim outcomes. Verify prior mined/rejected articles were skipped, blocked unread access remains retryable, and recovery reuses saved material. Check the repeated-listing/URL-alias deduplication proof: no duplicate read or terminal mining event. Distinct articles repeating the same thesis must link to the existing idea lineage.
- Read the task skill's [scoring rubric](../aoi-develop-ideas/references/idea-scoring.md). For every mined idea verify dimension evidence, 0–4 anchors, weights, contributions, total, band, eligibility gates, ranking/tie-breaks, and sensitivity. Recompute arithmetic. Unknown dimensions must remain unscored with incomplete total; points cannot compensate for an unread body, duplicate mining, unsupported required inputs or a fired falsifier. Scoring must not be represented as event probability or performance.
- Verify the discovery window and candidate/rejection log; confirm ranking is supported and existing ideas are deduplicated.
- Open material sources and check authorship, dates, primary corroboration, lawful access, source independence, and faithful claim extraction. Inaccessible decisive evidence blocks verification.
- Check the causal thesis, differentiated view, measurable success event, horizon, catalysts, falsifiers, strongest countercase, and equity mapping. A popular article alone is insufficient.
- Audit evidence direction/strength and likelihood rationale; distinguish event probability from confidence. Verify unsupported precision is identified.
- Recompute each scenario and expected return for today/later, probability totals, costs, currencies, dates, downside, and conditional/no-fill assumptions. Check missing data remains explicit.
- Inspect the root `INDEX.md` and one `ideas/<idea-id>/IDEA.md` per retained idea as the primary human-readable deliverable. Read the Markdown itself; a file-existence check is insufficient. Verify full idea coverage, no duplicates, stable paths, working relative index→idea and idea→index links, and readable company/ticker/title labels. The index must show as-of/status, thesis, risk/blocker and score or explicit incomplete subtotal/range; every idea page must stand alone without opening JSON or a manifest.
- Check browsing order against the authoritative helper output: complete eligible ideas sorted by total/evidence/downside, incomplete and ineligible/rejected ideas in separate labeled groups, incomplete subgroup by known subtotal descending with an explicit non-rank/non-comparability explanation. Missing dimensions must never be presented as a complete score or promoted by display order. Reconcile every displayed score, contribution, band, eligibility, status, source date and current-version reference with the underlying authority.
- On each idea page verify a concise takeaway; equity/horizon/measurable event and likelihood; named author/article provenance and clickable source links/locators; supporting and opposing evidence with strength/rationale; countercase/catalysts/falsifiers; six-dimension rubric with explanations; current today/later return assessment or specific unavailable-data reasons; and prioritized next checks. Important limitations must appear with the relevant score/return, not only in a distant audit file. Historical illustrative figures must be clearly separated from current estimates. Require plain readable prose and small focused tables; raw IDs/JSON/hash/ledger details must not dominate the main reading flow.
- Verify snapshot completeness and next research actions. Invented sources, omitted decisive counterevidence, or unsupported ROI require revision.

## TLDR acceptance rubric

- Enforce [the idea TLDR contract](../../docs/idea-tldr.md) on **every retained idea**, including incomplete/ineligible/invalidated items, in Markdown, structured current snapshot and exported/rendered output when supplied. Verify its leading placement and readability; all three parts must be understandable without opening JSON.
- Check that the source-derived thesis and falsifiable outcome have a bounded, anchored decision window; distinguish earliest informative check, final resolution and return holding period. Reject an unanchored “long term” or catalyst date presented as certain thesis resolution.
- Count at most three major supporting observations, each source-linked and causally explained. Verify roles, strength and independence; correlated repetitions are not independent support. Require a concise countercase/falsifier and no disguised fourth supporting argument.
- Reconcile **every** target instrument/share class/exchange/direction/currency. Check current reference marks against dated price receipts, session, adjustment basis and cutoff freshness; stale/failed coverage cannot be labeled current. Ratings require provider/type/value/scale/as-of and faithful source context. Article-author opinion cannot become Quant/consensus; AOI score/band/eligibility remains separate and is not success probability. Check available material ratings were actually sought; report specific gaps and next checks without fabrication.
- Independently recompute the predicted **annualized net total-return bear–bull range** from sourced current-entry inputs, scenario exit values, distributions, costs, FX conventions and positive actual holding days. Verify the 365/day exponent, downside, numeric endpoint ordering and distinction from cumulative ROI; later-entry ranges need their own duration/fill/no-fill conditions. A price-target upside is not an annual return. Invalidated ideas cannot retain an actionable current forecast.
- An unavailable field passes only the missing-data handling criterion when the gap/retrieval evidence is truthful, specific and visible with a next check, and unsupported/stale forecasts are suppressed. It does not establish complete source coverage or waive another required check. If supplied lawful sources support a calculation, blanket “unavailable” is a revision finding. In a declared offline fixture replay, judge freshness and source truth against the fixed fixture cutoff and label any pass as fixture-only; this cannot establish live market verification.
- Rate each TLDR dimension **pass / revise / blocked**: (1) thesis and decision window, (2) support/countercase, (3) target/price provenance, (4) ratings/AOI distinction, (5) annualized range arithmetic or justified gap, (6) readability and cross-artifact freshness. Record criterion-level evidence and calculation checks; every applicable dimension and the existing research gate must pass for an overall pass.

Output: artifact identity/hash, cutoff, checks and evidence, findings with repair actions, pass/revise/blocked verdict, and remaining limitations. A pass requires every applicable requirement above and in the contract; do not modify the artifact.
