# aoi-develop-ideas

Mine and assess ideas that authors actually present in Seeking Alpha's latest trending analysis. Do not originate investment theses independently or substitute another discovery source. Primary filings, price data, and contrary research validate or challenge a mined idea; they do not supply a new seed thesis.

## Inputs and persistent memory

Read [the research contract](../../docs/research-contract.md), [article memory](references/article-memory.md), and [idea scoring](references/idea-scoring.md). Load universe/constraints, prior ideas/learnings, and the persistent article ledger. Default exploratory scope is listed equities with no assumed personal allocation constraints. Unless the user configures another data root, use `/Volumes/Agent/AOI-data` on this machine; report the resolved location and preserve it across chats/runs. Never put runtime article history inside the skills repository. If a durable ledger cannot be opened, stop new mining and report the blocker; do not silently use an empty temporary ledger. Training may use an explicitly declared isolated ledger; that tests persistence within the training run, not production history.

## Source-opening preflight

Before opening any new article body, bind the existing durable ledger and inspect only the task/configuration, listing metadata, source index and prior processing state. Listing files or filenames may be inspected first; **do not bulk-read, recursively concatenate, or batch-open article-body files during input preparation**, including synthetic replay source files. For each article, resolve its ID and record the canonical helper's successful claim response before opening its body; if the claim is declined because it was mined/rejected, skip the body entirely. Then persist the extraction and read/result transitions. A later successful claim does not repair an earlier unrecorded body read: disclose the actual ordering as a process failure and retain it for evaluation.

For a local fixture or lawfully saved body, use `scripts/open_claimed_body.py --db <existing-ledger> --url <canonical-url> --run <id> --body-file <path> --receipts <private-run-receipts> --synthetic` (omit `--synthetic` outside fixtures). Read the body only from its successful output. The helper durably saves the real canonical claim response before accessing body bytes, then saves a linked access/hash receipt; declined claims never open the body. It does not mark an extraction read or a result mined: do those normal transitions after saving the actual extraction/result. Do not reopen the same source file separately to construct receipts. For live browser access, save the successful claim response before navigation and retain the actual body-opening tool/archive receipt, not a later recollection of the sequence.

## Discover and mine

1. Open the latest [Seeking Alpha Trending Analysis](https://seekingalpha.com/trending-analysis) listing, or the clearly identified Trending Analysis section of its homepage if the dedicated page fails. Save observation time, URL, visible order, canonical article URLs/IDs, title, author, available publication time, retrieval/access details, and listing evidence. Trending reflects what is observable at retrieval, not a historical ranking. Cached search snippets alone cannot establish the latest list; never invent that provenance. Keep every run's listing snapshot.
2. Resolve each article to its Seeking Alpha article ID, canonicalize URL aliases, and check the ledger **before reading its body or mining**. Skip previously mined or rejected articles, even if title, URL slug or trending position changes. Reuse saved results by reference. A different new article about the same equity may be read, but deduplicate the underlying idea and attach new evidence to its lineage rather than minting a duplicate thesis. Record all skips and reasons. Use the ledger helper's atomic claim to prevent concurrent runs from processing the same article.
3. Read only lawfully accessible article bodies (including the user's authorized subscription session if available). A title, teaser, snippet, or summary alone is insufficient to mine an idea. Record unavailable/partial access as blocked with the precise boundary, leave it eligible for a later lawful access retry, and do not fabricate article conclusions or replace the seed with your own thesis. If login is required, ask the user to sign in to Seeking Alpha in their browser or authorized session and confirm when ready; provide the site/article link. Never ask for passwords, verification codes or session tokens. Keep the access-dependent work pending while the user signs in, continue independent preparation, then verify access and retry the unread blocked articles through the ledger. If the user declines, cannot log in, or subscription access is still insufficient, record that specific limitation without bypassing it. A login request is required before concluding that login-blocked articles cannot be mined. No accessible eligible articles may legitimately yield zero new ideas; report that outcome and the access/duplicate coverage.
4. For an accessible article, record the reading and a durable extraction with author thesis, target equities/direction, mechanism, stated valuation, catalysts, risks, disclosures, source locators, and what the author did not specify. Persist the extraction before further work. Also save a concise source-access receipt from the actual rendered view: canonical URL, observation time, access state, thesis-bearing section locators and faithful limited excerpts or summaries, plus the tool/archive receipt locator. This receipt must come from observed body content, not from rewriting the extraction as proof. Exclude unrelated account/sidebar data and avoid archiving full paid articles. A later verification receipt is dated separately from the original reading and never creates another mining event. Mine all usable ideas from that article in this one processing event; link each idea back to article ID and exact supporting passages. Distinguish the author's assertions from AOI verification and AOI scenario assumptions. Do not turn an article's bearish thesis into an unrelated bullish thesis or add unmentioned equities as new seeds. An out-of-scope article gets a terminal rejection reason so it is not repeatedly mined.
5. Validate material claims with original evidence and seek the strongest countercase. Build falsifiable snapshots following the contract: source-derived thesis and variant, horizon and defined success event, evidence ledger with explained support/opposition, likelihood and uncertainty, target equities, today/later return scenarios, risks, falsifiers, and next checks. AOI may supply clearly labeled analytical assumptions needed to assess the author's idea; those assumptions must not become an invented seed thesis. Missing data stays unavailable; no quota forces a candidate into active status.
6. Apply [the claim-level evidence/dependency coverage check](references/evidence-coverage.md) before scoring. Score each mined idea with the fixed rubric, including evidence-linked component scores, weighted arithmetic, triage band and sensitivity. Keep unscored/ineligible ideas explicit; high popularity or author enthusiasm earns no automatic points. Generate score totals, bands, eligibility and ranks with `scripts/score_ideas.py` as specified in the rubric. Propagate unavailable required valuation/reference-price evidence into an unscored valuation dimension and incomplete total; retain historical illustrative arithmetic separately. Rejected, incomplete or evidence-gated candidates must have null rank in every artifact. Derive all current score/rank fields from the generated table, use the declared tie-breaks and explain selection/rejection.
7. Persist article processing outcome and idea/extraction paths transactionally with the helper. Mark `mined` only after durable idea records exist; record `rejected` when no usable idea is extracted, with a reason. Preserve crash recovery state: resume from saved extraction/results without rereading or creating another mining event. Memory commits survive later evaluation failure; improve the representation from saved material rather than mine the article again.
8. Publish the readable idea library described below as the primary deliverable, with a ranked shortlist, article-to-idea provenance map, component scoring table, rejected/blocked/duplicate log, source coverage and gaps, snapshot paths, and ledger location plus before/after evidence. A second pass over the same listing must skip completed articles and create zero duplicate mining events. Request `aoi-develop-ideas-eval` on the frozen output before treating it as vetted.

## Source extraction and decision checks

- Classify sources by actual role: issuer management commentary, independent analyst opinion, reported fact, or AOI inference. Prepared earnings remarks remain issuer commentary. Record visible holdings, sponsorship/subscription incentives, and other conflicts as self-disclosures separately from independently verified positions or track records.
- Give each material claim a precise page/section/table/row locator; verify PDF versus printed pagination and split claims that occur in different places. Keep source manifest and evidence ledger consistent. Unsupported precise publication timestamps remain unknown.
- Bridge reported financial anchors to scenario earnings/cash flow and valuations. Explain idea-specific weights and uncertainty, and show sensitivity. Use a range or not estimable when a point probability lacks a defensible basis. ROI is a conditional model output, not validated performance.
- Reconcile narrative, snapshots, article ledger, scoring, citations and calculations. Do not erase contrary evidence or an available disclosure. Preserve original extraction and forecast history, and log any later correction as a new version.

## Provenance clocks and current-version references

- Record `generated_at` separately from each source's actual `retrieved_at`, market `trade_at`, publication time, and event period. Populate retrieval times from original tool/archive receipts, never from the artifact builder's clock. If a source's retrieval timestamp cannot be recovered, mark it unknown and retain the available receipt/time bounds; do not invent a precise value. A retrieval bound also needs an actual source observation receipt; a builder timestamp, ledger claim/rejection time or unrelated snapshot cannot establish when content was retrieved. If no source-specific receipt supports a bound, leave the bound unknown. The actual retrieval cutoff is the latest supported source retrieval, not a snapshot creation time; disclose when a complete cutoff cannot be reconstructed. Reconcile these clocks across the narrative, source/evidence manifests, quotes, and any CSV exports before freezing.
- Maintain an explicit current-snapshot index. After an append-only correction, resolve every current candidate/score/narrative pointer to the latest valid snapshot and verify the referenced fields agree. Retain earlier snapshots and immutable article-ledger result references as history; distinguish original mining references from current presentation pointers. Build the current index from version lineage rather than copying a stale path. Recheck all current pointers and timestamp invariants after final generation.

## Resolve evidence dependencies before freezing

For each current rubric dimension, catalyst, and falsifier, identify the exact facts and evidence IDs it depends on. Check that those sources currently support those facts with accessible receipts or dated lawful verification. A source marked historical, unavailable or unverified cannot silently become current support elsewhere. Ground the field in supported evidence and reassess it, or leave it unscored/conditional and state what must be verified. Apply this across all dimensions, not just valuation; propagate changes through every current snapshot, score input/output, index and narrative. A falsifier based on an unverified target must explicitly remain conditional pending target verification, rather than implying a validated issuer commitment.

When material verification is attainable within the authorized read-only scope, attempt it before concluding evidence is unavailable. New verification of an existing source claim is allowed and dated separately; it is not new idea mining and must not overwrite the original receipt or forecast. Use precise source locators and preserve failed access attempts. A missing local archive alone does not prove a currently accessible original source cannot be checked. Do not fill gaps by relying on old evaluation verdicts or another performer's summary.

## Required TLDR

Read and follow [the idea TLDR contract](../../docs/idea-tldr.md). Lead every retained idea with its three-part TLDR and store matching structured `tldr` in the current snapshot. Retrieve current reference-price and named rating evidence when accessible; missing fields require specific gaps and next checks. Show the modeled annualized net bear–bull return range using actual holding days, distinct from cumulative upside. Never fill a range from a stale mark or unsupported model.

## Readable idea library

Manage every retained idea in its own stable folder: `<data-root>/ideas/<idea-id>/IDEA.md`. Put `INDEX.md` at the output/data root. During training use the assigned iteration output root; after a passing evaluation publish the same library at the configured normal data root. Preserve immutable snapshots and history; `IDEA.md` is the current human-readable view. Keep machine-readable records and raw receipts available as supporting material, without making them prerequisites for reading the ideas.

`INDEX.md` is the browsing entry point. Start with the as-of date, coverage, and a short explanation of score/status. Link every retained idea by readable company/ticker/title and use relative paths so the library remains navigable after copying. Include status, score or explicit incomplete subtotal/range, headline thesis, main blocker/risk, and last updated time. Put complete eligible ideas first, sorted by the rubric's total/evidence/downside tie-breaks. Put incomplete and ineligible/rejected ideas in clearly labeled separate groups. Within the incomplete group, sort by known subtotal descending for browsing only; state that missing dimensions make these subtotals incomparable to complete scores and that display order is not investment rank. Show every idea exactly once. Do not silently drop a weak, incomplete or out-of-scope retained idea; article-only rejections may be summarized in coverage rather than presented as invented ideas.

Each `IDEA.md` links back to the root `INDEX.md` and must be self-contained for a human reader, using plain prose, short sections and small tables:

- Lead with company/ticker, status, as-of, and a short takeaway: what the source-derived idea is, why it could work, why it could fail, and what to do next in research. Explain abbreviations when first used.
- Give equity/exchange/currency/direction, time horizon, measurable success event, event likelihood/trend or explicit not-estimable status. Keep event probability separate from scores.
- Attribute the seed thesis to the named Seeking Alpha author/article with a clickable URL, publication/observation dates and readable section locators. Distinguish source assertions, corroborated facts, and AOI assumptions.
- Summarize supporting and opposing evidence with strength and rationale, catalysts, and falsifiers. Link material facts to original sources/locators directly in Markdown; opaque evidence IDs alone are insufficient. Present the strongest countercase prominently.
- Show six rubric dimensions, weight, score or unscored, contribution and concise idea-specific rationale. Show authoritative total/band/eligibility or explicit incomplete known subtotal/range; do not turn the subtotal into a full score. Explain the main scoring uncertainty.
- Give today/later return assessment, bear/base/bull/expected values when supported, conditions/horizon/cost/dividend assumptions and no-fill caveat; when unavailable say why and what is needed. Any historical illustrative model must be clearly separated from current estimates and must not dominate the opening summary.
- End with prioritized next research checks, last updated/source cutoff, and optional links to current snapshot/history/receipts. Readers must not need JSON or an audit manifest to understand the idea, score, risk, or next action. Do not dump field names, hashes or ledger mechanics into the main reading flow.

Generate the Markdown index and idea pages from the same current snapshot/score authority as machine-readable artifacts. Check all relative links, one-page-per-idea coverage, score/status/as-of consistency and index ordering before freezing. Keep layouts and stable paths on future updates so the index remains the fast way to browse by score.

## Final TLDR provenance checks

Keep each date, number and causal assertion at the precision and attribution supported by its cited passage. A model's assumed fill date is not a verified catalyst date; a month's guidance does not establish an exact day. Author opinion or a proposed falsifier must cite the author/model rather than be attributed to an issuer that did not state it. If the author did not specify a market-consensus variant, say it is unestablished rather than inventing the author's view.

Validate the public export as well as the private library. Retain the index's score/status/as-of/risk fields, incomplete/ineligible grouping and browsing-order explanation; do not replace it with bare ticker links. Resolve structured TLDR source/model locators from their containing snapshot and rewrite/check them after export. Missing rating categories and AOI score fields need a specific next check in the TLDR itself. Store success-event probabilities on the contract's 0–100 percent scale, with explicit units; keep subjective scenario weights separate.

A configured exit-price file alone establishes scenario arithmetic, not verified valuation inputs. Require an evidence-grounded operating/cash-flow-to-valuation bridge and relevant sensitivity before asserting valuation is verified or making a current predicted-return/ranking claim. If that bridge is missing, keep valuation unscored, ranking disabled and the current predicted range unavailable with a next check; any retained scenario arithmetic must be labeled hypothetical and its limitation shown beside it in the TLDR. Do not turn missing analyst consensus into an invented mispricing claim.

Build and inspect the actual interactive reader ordering: the TLDR must precede detailed scoring. Keep the horizon human-readable; if the machine horizon is structured, provide/render a readable decision window rather than dumping an object. Validate the site Markdown download's rebased links as well as its HTML, snapshot and source/model links.

Validate every separately packaged library at its own root, not only the main output/public/site: include the calculation and concise source records its pages reference, or rebase those links to stable supported locations. Check each bundle's Markdown and snapshot references independently after packaging. Do not claim link integrity based on a different copy of the library.

## Complete freeze boundary

At the assigned training/data root, create the actual primary `INDEX.md` and `ideas/<id>/IDEA.md` paths before making optional nested copies. A wrapper index that only links to another library does not satisfy the root deliverable.

Maintain a real evidence ledger, not just a source list. For supporting **and opposing** claims include stable evidence ID, publisher/author, publication/retrieval/event clocks (explicitly unknown where not supported), exact source locator, direction, strength with rationale, reliability, independence group and superseded/retracted state. Bind each scored dimension, catalyst and falsifier to its exact fact/evidence IDs. Explain evidence strength briefly in the readable idea page as well as the machine ledger. Freeze the actual claim/body-opening receipts with the outputs; a narrative that points to an unavailable parent transcript cannot establish access chronology.

Before recording a source extraction as read, audit every attributed field against that article's actual body. Preserve each catalyst/check date at the source's stated precision and distinguish it from AOI review deadlines, later-entry assumptions and hypothetical fill dates. Populate the canonical decision window from these audited source fields, then derive **both** Markdown and structured TLDR from that same window. Recheck current snapshots and all public/site copies; correcting prose alone leaves a false structured forecast. Link each extraction only to that article's claim/access receipts, checking the claim's article ID and the access receipt's body path/hash, rather than attaching every receipt from the run.

Run `scripts/check_library_refs.py <output-root>` after all export/site steps and fix every reported local reference, including source/model locators inside copied JSON under `site/files/`. Run it separately for any standalone bundle too. Public-safe source Markdown that a downloaded snapshot references must exist at that snapshot-relative location, or its structured locator must be changed to a supported external source. This audit verifies reference integrity only; it is not an evaluator pass or source-truth check.

Before freezing, reconcile every material source-dependent statement in extraction, TLDR, model and score rationale with a claim-level ledger entry, including author timing/success/falsifier definitions, price/implementation observations, ratings and AOI model assumptions. A document in the source manifest does not substitute for those evidence records. Validate the source roles and locators for each dimension's own dependencies; do not paste the same operating-ID list into unrelated dimensions. Preserve declared synthetic/fixture-only scope and source cutoff in every standalone exported snapshot as well as its page.

Use the repository’s canonical `scripts/export_idea_library.py` and `scripts/build_idea_site.py` (resolved from the repository root, not this skill’s scripts directory) for public packaging and the interactive reader. Do not replace the reader with a hand-written line-by-line Markdown-to-paragraph converter: it loses table semantics and existing navigation. Inspect the generated TLDR and score sections for semantic `<table>` markup (or deliberately designed labeled cards), then preview both complete and incomplete ideas through localhost. Literal header/separator pipes in rendered paragraphs are a presentation failure; fix the reusable renderer or input format before freezing. Record the actual build command and renderer path/hash.

Before freezing, compare structured and human claims field by field, including the countercase: split a mixed issuer fact/author threshold into separately attributed claims and cite each exact passage in both forms. Put clickable quote and rating-panel/public-safe receipt links beside the human TLDR values; a source URL available only in JSON is insufficient. Derive all displayed holding-day counts from the same date-subtraction calculation used for annualization, including later-entry prose, instead of retyping them. In evidence/provenance/clock manifests propagate known document publication timestamps to every dependent claim; use unknown only after checking the source header/receipt. Audit these fields across original extraction, current Markdown/snapshot and every exported copy.

Audit snapshot completeness against the research contract’s full field list before freezing; a compliant TLDR alone is insufficient. Store explicit `scenarios`, `entry_conditions` and `next_review` records, with current/later status and specific unsupported reasons/actions where needed. Link actual calculation sidecars using resolvable containing-file-relative paths, even when retaining hypothetical calculations separately from unavailable current forecasts. The next research review needs a date or actionable condition distinct from the thesis decision window. Audit each idea’s score rationale against only that idea’s sources and 0–4 anchor; never reuse another target’s risks, and reassess rather than assign an anchor unsupported by bounded stress evidence.

In a constrained output directory, stage a separate export source at `<assigned-output>/export-source` and export to `<assigned-output>/public`, then build `<assigned-output>/site`. These source and public directories satisfy the exporter’s disjoint-root constraint while staying inside the authorized output boundary. Resolve and check every planned write path before invoking a helper; do not temporarily export to a sibling outside the assigned directory and move it afterward.

After export, compare each standalone public/downloadable snapshot’s full required research fields with the current authority, not only its TLDR: scenarios, entry conditions, next review, execution status, variant perception, success event, evidence IDs, catalysts/falsifiers and lineage must survive. Stage public-safe prior snapshots and actual scenario/model evidence sidecars as needed so these retained references resolve from the exported copy; never expose private ledger/access records. Canonical exporter preserves the declared research schema and follows staged local prior-snapshot lineage; verify the generated copies rather than assuming a successful build establishes schema completeness.

Finalize the canonical claim ledger before deriving dependent artifacts. After splitting or correcting a claim, regenerate affected snapshot evidence IDs, score/catalyst/falsifier dependencies, source and clock manifests and all exported copies; a corrected ledger alone is insufficient. Check each cited section actually contains that claim, including author disclosures, and rewrite inherited strength/affected-assumption text when splitting roles. Freeze one immutable snapshot identity at creation: use exclusive-create or verify identical existing bytes; if a rerun changes any field (including created_at), append a new version with prior_snapshot instead of overwriting. Export the exact current identity from that single authority and compare all shared fields/creation timestamps across private/staged/public/site copies. Rebuilding presentation must not regenerate or overwrite research snapshots.

Each TLDR support must explain a causal chain in plain language: observed fact → economic/operating driver → relevance to the source thesis, conditional on the stated uncertainty. Add a concise strength judgment with the reason for its limit. “The source supports this claim” or “no live validation” describes provenance, not a causal explanation or evidence strength. Derive the matching structured rationale from the same text. A pending decision, unresolved dependency or rejection risk belongs under catalyst/countercase unless its positive information content is separately justified; it is not automatically a supporting observation.

After the final current-version selection, regenerate every index row and provenance/current-version description from that current authority, including readable last-updated fields. Compare timestamps, version names and paths in root/staged/public indexes, IDEA footers, current index JSON, provenance and exported snapshots after the last correction. Original/mining creation clocks and current presentation clocks are distinct, and no stale version may be described as current.

Audit `event_period` separately from publication/retrieval/processing clocks for every timing, success, milestone and catalyst claim. Extract the date, month, quarter or bounded relative window actually stated in that claim’s source and preserve its precision in evidence and clock manifests, including future events and author-defined resolution windows. A future event need not have occurred to have a known stated period. Do not populate all event periods with a generic unknown: use unknown only when that source truly gives no period, and ensure the ledger agrees with the canonical decision window and readable timing text.

Use a source registry with the actual named publisher and author from each receipt/header; propagate those identities into every claim. A role such as “issuer”, “author” or “fixture source” belongs in a separate role field and cannot replace available names. The readable idea’s source attribution must show the seed article’s known publication date as well as observation/as-of dates. Audit strength/rationale/affected-assumption text claim by claim for copied language from another issuer or source; common formatting may be reused, but business-risk explanations must describe that specific claim and its source.

Create research snapshots through [the immutable JSON writer](scripts/write_snapshot.py) (`--input <draft-json> --output <new-snapshot>`, or import its `write_snapshot(path, value)` function). Identical retries preserve the original bytes; any changed value requires a new version and prior_snapshot lineage. Do not leave a generic overwrite-capable research builder as the snapshot creation path.

Keep comparison-period precision faithful: a known current quarter and an unspecified “prior” comparator do not establish that the comparison was the immediately preceding quarter. Author positions, incentives and no-position disclosures are conflict/quality metadata with neutral or cautionary relevance; they are not independent corroboration or positive thesis evidence. Set direction/role/independence/strength rationale accordingly. Validate downloaded JSON scenario/calculation dependencies at the exact site/files location; a working original/public link does not establish a working download.
