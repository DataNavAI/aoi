## Strongest countercase

The strongest countercase is that low valuation multiples may reflect peak earnings. Customer coverage is partial, deposits can be returned on performance, and letters of credit have contingent recovery. Renegotiation, delayed demand, memory economizing or supplier substitution can weaken the promised protection.
