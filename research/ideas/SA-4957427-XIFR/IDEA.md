## Strongest countercase

The strongest countercase is that a high pre-growth cash-flow yield is not cash distributable to units. Corporate/project debt, financing obligations and restricted subsidiary distributions can consume the apparent yield. Refinancing or dilution can destroy residual unit value even while operating assets generate cash.
